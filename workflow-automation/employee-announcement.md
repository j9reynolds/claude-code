# Draft: Staff Announcement (send before any automation goes live)

Recommended before the first automation ships. It sets expectations honestly, invites the
people who know the toil best to point at it, and keeps the program at the process level.

---

**Subject: We're mapping our processes to take the busywork off your plate**

Team,

Over the next few weeks we're taking a careful look at how work actually flows through our
tools — McLeod, Outlook, our accounting systems, the CRM — to find the repetitive,
time-eating steps that software can take over. Think: the detention/accessorial approval
emails, the copy-paste between the bank and the spreadsheets, filing PODs, assembling the
same reports every month.

Two things I want to be clear about:

1. **This is about processes, not people.** We're looking at *what steps happen and where
   they get stuck*, not at how any individual spends their day. No screen monitoring, no
   keystroke tracking, nothing like that.

2. **The goal is to give you time back**, not to replace judgment. Anything that touches
   money or goes out to a customer or carrier will still have a person approving it — the
   automation just does the setup and the math so you don't have to.

**The fastest way to help:** you already know where the busywork is. Reply to this note,
or drop it in [channel/form], with the answer to one question:

> *What do you do every week that you wish a computer would just do for you?*

We'll prioritize the list, start with a few quick, low-risk wins, and keep you posted on
what's shipping.

Thanks,
[Name]
